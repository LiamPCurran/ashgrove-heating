FAQ

Common questions

Can't find your answer? Call (613) 555-0142 and talk to a real person.

We service all major brands, including Lennox, Carrier, Trane, Goodman, Mitsubishi, Daikin, Rheem and Napoleon.

Do you charge for estimates?

Quotes for new furnaces, heat pumps and air conditioners are free. For repairs there's a flat diagnostic fee, and we tell you the repair price before any work starts.

How fast can you come when the heat is out?

No-heat calls get priority, 24/7 during the heating season. Most are reached the same day.

Should I repair my furnace or replace it?

If the furnace is under about 15 years old and the repair is a fraction of a new unit, repair usually wins. We'll show you the numbers both ways and let you decide.

Do heat pumps work in an Ottawa winter?

Modern cold-climate heat pumps keep heating well below −20°C. Most Ottawa homes pair one with a furnace or electric backup for the coldest nights.

Do you offer financing?

Yes. New systems can be financed with monthly payments, on approved credit. We'll show you the monthly figure alongside the upfront price.

Which brands do you service?

All major furnace, heat pump and air conditioner brands. We're not tied to one manufacturer, so we recommend whatever fits your home and budget.
