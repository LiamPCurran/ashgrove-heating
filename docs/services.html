Heating and cooling services

Everything a typical Ottawa home needs to stay warm in January and cool in July.

Furnace repair and replacement

We service all major gas furnace brands. Most no-heat problems come down to a short list of parts: igniters, flame sensors, pressure switches and blower motors. Our technicians carry the common ones, so most repairs are finished on the first visit.

If replacement makes more sense, we'll do a proper heat-loss calculation and quote two or three options at different price points. More about furnace repair →

Heat pump installation

Cold-climate air-source heat pumps can heat and cool your home and cut your gas bill. We'll tell you honestly whether one suits your house, what it will cost and which rebates may apply. More about heat pumps →

Air conditioning

Central AC installation, repairs and spring tune-ups. If you're replacing an older AC, it's worth asking about a heat pump instead. It cools the same way and heats too.
