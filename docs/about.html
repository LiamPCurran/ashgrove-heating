A heating company that tells you the truth

Ashgrove started with a simple frustration: too many homeowners were being sold new furnaces they didn't need. A cracked igniter would become a $7,000 replacement because that's what paid the best commission.

We work differently. Our technicians aren't paid on commission. Their job is to find the real problem, explain it in plain English and give you options. Sometimes that means a new system. More often it means a $180 part and a clean filter.

What you can expect from us

  • A firm price before work starts. If we find something else, we stop and ask.
  • Licensed, background-checked technicians who wear boot covers and use drop cloths.
  • A short written summary of every visit: what we found, what we did and what to watch for.
  • A phone that gets answered, by a person, including on cold January nights.

Where we work

We're an Ottawa company serving homes from Kanata and Stittsville to Orléans, and from Manotick up to Centretown. See the full list on our home page.
