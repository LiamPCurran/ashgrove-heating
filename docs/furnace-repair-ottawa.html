Before you call: three quick checks

1Thermostat

Set to "Heat", a few degrees above room temperature, with fresh batteries.

2Filter

A clogged filter can make the furnace overheat and shut itself off. Swap it if it's grey.

3Power switch

The light-style switch near the furnace should be on, and the breaker shouldn't be tripped.

Still nothing? Call us. Don't keep resetting it.

Problems we fix every week

  • Furnace won't ignite or keeps clicking
  • Burners light, then shut off (often the flame sensor)
  • Blowing cold air
  • Loud banging, squealing or rattling
  • Short cycling: on and off constantly
  • Error codes or a blinking status light

Repair or replace?

We'll give you our honest opinion, with numbers. As a rough guide:

  • Repair if the furnace is under ~15 years old and the fix is a small fraction of a new unit.
  • Consider replacing if it's 18+ years old, needs a major part, or has needed several repairs in two winters.

Thinking about replacing? Ask about a heat pump or dual-fuel system.

Furnace repair FAQ

Why is my furnace running but blowing cold air?

Common causes are a dirty flame sensor, a failed igniter, a clogged filter causing the furnace to overheat and shut off its burners, or a thermostat set to 'fan on'. Check the filter and thermostat first; if that doesn't fix it, call us.

How much does furnace repair cost?

Most common repairs, such as a flame sensor, igniter or pressure switch, run roughly $150 to $450 including the diagnostic. Blower motors and control boards cost more. You get the exact price before we start.

Is it safe to keep resetting my furnace?

No. If it keeps locking out, something is wrong, and repeated resets can mask a safety problem. If you ever smell gas, leave the house and call your gas utility's emergency line first.
